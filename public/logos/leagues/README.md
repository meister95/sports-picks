# Liga-Logos

- Premier League: crowned lion-head logo from [FootyLogos](https://www.footylogos.com/logos/premier-league-england), placed on an optically centered transparent square. Their page requests source credit; the site footer links to FootyLogos.
- Bundesliga: [current logo vector](https://logotyp.us/file/bundesliga.svg), locally cropped to its artwork so the player and wordmark fill the fixed logo tile.
- La Liga: [2023 vertical logo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LaLiga_2023_Vertical_Logo.svg).
- Champions League: [UEFA Champions League icon](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:UEFA_Champions_League_logo_no_text_great.svg).
- Europa League: 2024 logo from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:UEFA_Europa_League_logo_(2024_version).svg), cropped to the full cup and wordmark and optimized locally as PNG for display.

League logos are marks of their respective rights holders.
